Terms of service

What we provide

TY Signatures provides hairdressing, beauty treatment and cosmetics retail services provided by TY SIGNATURES, a company incorporated under Belgian law and registered with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises under enterprise number 1022.105.727. Hairdressing is a regulated trade in Belgium and the business operates under the required proof of professional competence, held under [PROOF OF PROFESSIONAL COMPETENCE]. A patch test is carried out at least 48 hours before any colour, bleach, lash tint or brow tint service, as product manufacturers require, and such services are declined where no valid patch test has been done, including for returning clients whose previous test is out of date or where the product has changed; reactions to these products can be severe and previous uneventful use is not protection. A consultation and contraindication check is completed before a first treatment, and clients must disclose pregnancy, skin conditions, recent facial treatments or peels, use of retinoids or acids, and any medication affecting skin or healing; a treatment may be declined or modified on safety grounds. Tools are cleaned and sterilised between clients and single-use items are disposed of after one use. Cosmetic products offered for sale carry the labelling required by the European cosmetic products regulation, including the full ingredient list; treatments and products are cosmetic and no claim is made to diagnose, treat or cure any skin, scalp or medical condition. Services are described on the Services page. No physical goods are sold or shipped through this site.
