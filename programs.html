Program 02 · No test, no colour

Colour

We will also be honest about what colour can do in one appointment. Going several shades lighter on previously dyed hair is a process over sittings, not a single visit, and a salon promising it in one go is either guessing or about to damage your hair.

  • 1.5 to 4 hours
  • Adults
  • Per service
  • Valid patch test

Program 03 · Contraindications checked

Beauty treatments

Retinoids, acids and recent peels make skin lift during waxing and change how a facial should be done, so we ask what you are using. A facial is cosmetic: it cannot treat acne, rosacea or eczema, and we will say so and point you to a pharmacist or doctor instead of selling a course.

  • 30 to 75 minutes
  • Adults
  • Per treatment
  • Disclose products used
